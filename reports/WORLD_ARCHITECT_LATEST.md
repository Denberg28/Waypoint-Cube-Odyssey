# Waypoint World Architect — Latest Council Resolution

Decision: **EXPAND**

Expanding the world map by introducing a new planned route 'Sunken Grotto' off the western corridor (connecting Gloomwood Hollow and Lantern Crossing) to address exploratory engagement and collector demand for aquatic/subterranean biomes.

## Council basis
- Explorer & Collector and First-Time Player personas highlight demand for rich route discovery, collection ledgers, and immersive variety.
- Aggregate telemetry shows steady route traffic across western zones (Lantern Crossing and Gloomwood Hollow), validating regional expansion.
- Meets all cartographic constraints: connects cleanly to existing nodes without coordinate overlaps.

## Map action
- Added planned region **Sunken Grotto** (`sunken_grotto`)
- Difficulty concept: 2
- Theme: subterranean crystal pools
- Connections: gloomwood, treasure
- Landmark: The Luminescent Cascade
- Hazard: Slick Algae Slopes
- Collectible: Prismatic Pearls
- Status remains **planned / non-playable** until a later core-route promotion step validates gameplay support.

> Guardrail: the World Architect may expand the planning graph but cannot delete or rewrite existing playable routes and cannot promote a proposal to playable by itself.
