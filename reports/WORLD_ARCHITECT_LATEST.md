# Waypoint World Architect — Latest Council Resolution

Decision: **EXPAND**

Expanding the world map by adding a new planned region, 'Cinder Caldera', connected to Bramble Forge and Whispering Fen to satisfy combat and exploration demand for higher-difficulty volcanic hazard zones.

## Council basis
- Combat Challenger requested tighter encounter spacing and higher-difficulty challenge zones.
- Explorer & Collector demanded new route discoveries, dynamic revisit incentives, and unique collectible goals.
- Aggregate public telemetry shows strong engagement across adventure routes (forge, fen, fen variants), indicating readiness for a new volcanic pathway.

## Map action
- Added planned region **Cinder Caldera** (`cinder_caldera`)
- Difficulty concept: 4
- Theme: volcanic rift and magma flows
- Connections: forge, fen
- Landmark: The Obsidian Hearth
- Hazard: Magma Vents
- Collectible: Magma Ember Shards
- Status remains **planned / non-playable** until a later core-route promotion step validates gameplay support.

> Guardrail: the World Architect may expand the planning graph but cannot delete or rewrite existing playable routes and cannot promote a proposal to playable by itself.
