# Waypoint World Architect — Latest Council Resolution

Decision: **EXPAND**

Expanding the world map by adding 'Galecrest Spire', a planned high-difficulty vertical mountain region that connects Frostfang Pass and Whispering Fen. This satisfies the Explorer & Collector demand for route discovery and collection milestones while offering a fresh alpine biome with distinctive hazards and collectibles.

## Council basis
- Explorer & Collector requested an in-game collection ledger and new completion milestones across diverse exploration paths.
- Combat Challenger requested higher-difficulty engagement zones and density scaling to test mastery.
- Route telemetry indicates heavy traffic on high-difficulty paths like Whispering Fen and Frostfang Pass, pointing to strong demand for upper-tier geographical expansion.

## Map action
- Added planned region **Galecrest Spire** (`galecrest_spire`)
- Difficulty concept: 4
- Theme: alpine winds and ancient ruins
- Connections: frost, fen
- Landmark: The Whispering Summit Gate
- Hazard: Gale-Force Wind Gusts
- Collectible: Skyfeather Relics
- Status remains **planned / non-playable** until a later core-route promotion step validates gameplay support.

> Guardrail: the World Architect may expand the planning graph but cannot delete or rewrite existing playable routes and cannot promote a proposal to playable by itself.
